Capstone Project Proposal

In my capstone project, I will be exploring the uncanniness of human-AI interactions through an interactive visual novel. I was most inspired by the utopian/dystopian narrative lens we explored in class, which brought to mind some of my favorite examples that explore this theme of human-machine relationships. Most memorable to me is The Sandman by ETA Hoffman, which explores a sort of personal dystopia where the narrator is fooled to fall in love with a machine and is driven to madness by the realization of his lover’s inhumanity. As AI-generated conversation becomes increasingly difficult to distinguish from human conversation, it’s interesting to explore how our interactions with AI might lead us towards our own interpersonal dystopias. I would like to challenge the commonly imagined future where human to AI interaction becomes indistinguishable from human to human interaction through a speculative lens as well – what would happen if we designed these machines to be intentionally machine like? What if they were intentionally frustrating to use? How might that affect the kinds of relationships we develop with them? My vision aims to challenge the user to evaluate the relationship they have with the human-like technologies they use every day.

The structure of the project will be derived from the traditional visual novel, which is a video game style that emphasizes text-heavy narratives with still visuals to accompany. I plan to develop multiple characters that a user can interact with using dialogue options that will then determine how the character continues to present itself. This could be achieved using conditional logic that directs the program to a different branch depending on the option selected by the user, or by using a variable that is updated with each user input like we used in Game of Pig in DES 157A. I am also curious to see how the user behavior might change depending on how the character presents itself, and am interested in using Back4App to track user input to be evaluated later. JQuery UI also has many pre-built interfaces for collecting and using user input that would be useful for this project as well.

My target audience will be younger, digital-native generations that are used to the high degree of involvement big tech has in our day to day lives and relationships. For many of us, it’s normal for the world to be rapidly changing as new technology developes – from the internet, to social media, and now to AI – and we generally accept it without much protest. I think it’s important to engage with new technologies with a healthy level of skepticism and intentionality, and my goal for this project is to challenge digital-native groups to engage with tech with greater mindfulness.
